ClermontCyclistCoach

A cycling coaching app, currently in early development.

What it is

ClermontCyclistCoach (CCC) is a coaching app for cyclists. It is being built to help riders plan training and understand how each ride compared with what was planned.

Current status

CCC is in early development and is not yet open to the public. At this stage it only lets an account holder connect their Strava and Intervals.icu accounts. Training plan and ride analysis features are not built yet.

What it will access

  • Intervals.icu: read activities and wellness data; write planned workouts to your calendar.
  • Strava: read your activities and profile.

You can disconnect either service at any time. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is stored and how.
